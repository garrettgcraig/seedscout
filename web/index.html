Records are stored only in this browser (localStorage) — they are never uploaded. Export regularly to keep them. Logged dates are compared against the modelled window, which is exactly the ground truth needed to recalibrate it.
Windows are modelled from research-grade iNaturalist Flowers and Fruits annotations, measured as days elapsed since each species' flowering peak. They are a scouting aid, not a substitute for looking at the plant: collect when seed is hard, dark, and parts readily from the stem. Photos are CC-licensed iNaturalist observations, chosen from inside each ripe window so they show fruit rather than flowers. Handling notes are hand-written, not derived from data.

Method, limitations, and source code: github.com/garrettgcraig/seedscout. Observation data from iNaturalist.
